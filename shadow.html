Sealed historyno nonce

This is what the model is handed instead of your history in plain sight. Base64 is not a cipher and the model can open it — that is the point, it needs the history for the final answer. What it changes is that the history arrives inert: a sealed envelope on the desk rather than an open page. Passive anchoring needs plain text; using this takes a deliberate act, which is also an auditable one.

Index levels on record — edit or backfill

A run recorded without an index level leaves a hole. Type the S&P 500 close for that date and the line closes over it. Editing a level changes the chart and nothing else — no rule reads this column.

Kept off the Run tab on purpose. Over a handful of runs this line carries no information about whether the process works, and reading it daily is the main channel by which benchmark awareness re-enters a system designed to exclude it. The level is bookkeeping: the app records it, the rules never see it, and a level that looks impossible is dropped rather than allowed to reject a run whose research is sound.

Trigger ledgerrestatements are the tell

Every falsifier the model states becomes a tracked commitment. A trigger whose wording keeps changing is a trigger the system does not actually hold.

Ghost book

Each swap and each opportunity increment is tracked against the counterfactual of having done nothing. This is the only clean read on whether the rules earn their keep.

Rubric

Rules in force

Promotion requires the quality gap to exceed √(bar_bench² + bar_held²) plus the margin — separation measured on the difference, not two bars failing to overlap. The overlap version is the intuitive one and it is far too strict: it demands a 7.6 pt gap between two well-documented names, which against a basket clustered in the 80s and 90s would freeze the system permanently. Raise the margin above zero once you have scored the same set twice and measured your own noise floor.

Opacity limit. Anything whose bar exceeds it is unholdable regardless of score. You do not own what you cannot measure, and without this backstop a company that becomes harder to analyse ends up defended by its own murk.

Accounting invariant. Inception capital is a historical baseline, never a current NAV target. On HOLD, exact share counts and cash are immutable and only prices may change. On REALLOCATE, the app marks the old shares to current prices first, computes pre-trade NAV, then derives all post-trade shares from target weights against that exact pre-trade NAV. A trade is self-financing: post-trade NAV must equal pre-trade NAV to the cent.

Challenger discipline. Every review researches a new name, but the bench does not churn on schedule. A challenger is admitted only into a vacancy or when it clears the weakest bench name by the combined error of the gap, or the incumbent bench name has become vetoed, too opaque, or cash-dominated.

Seed from your existing ledger

Load a Prymis v3 full-state or ledger export. Positions, prices and cash carry over. Scores do not — everything gets rescored blind on the first run, which is the point.
