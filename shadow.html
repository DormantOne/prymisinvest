Rubric

Rules in force

Promotion requires the quality gap to exceed √(bar_bench² + bar_held²) plus the margin — separation measured on the difference, not two bars failing to overlap. The overlap version is the intuitive one and it is far too strict: it demands a 7.6 pt gap between two well-documented names, which against a basket clustered in the 80s and 90s would freeze the system permanently. Raise the margin above zero once you have scored the same set twice and measured your own noise floor.

Opacity limit. Anything whose bar exceeds it is unholdable regardless of score. You do not own what you cannot measure, and without this backstop a company that becomes harder to analyse ends up defended by its own murk.
